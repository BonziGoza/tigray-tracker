Publisher registry & credibility scoring

Each publisher has a provisional, reviewable profile based on accuracy and corrections (30%), sourcing and verification (25%), transparency (20%), editorial independence (15%), and track record (10%). Scores are newsroom-level baselines, not political-alignment ratings or probabilities that a specific claim is true.

Article credibility combines publisher reputation (30%), article-specific evidence (50%), and independent corroboration (up to 20 points), with explicit adjustments for disputed or party-origin claims. If article evidence could not be extracted, the article receives a neutral evidence baseline rather than an automatic penalty. Established publisher profiles are not scored below 50 solely because extraction is missing; assessed weak evidence can still lower the score.

Unassessed means insufficient information, not unreliable. Syndicated copies should not count as independent confirmation. Profiles are initial manual assessments and will be reviewed as outlet-specific evidence is added.
